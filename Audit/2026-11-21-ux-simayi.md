Review ID `AUD-002`. SimaYi reviewed placement, round controls, and the HUD from source. The runner could not use Chromium, so the touch sizes are CSS estimates and the audit did not verify live touch behavior. KongMing handled the separately requested attacker health and projectile changes.

| ID | Severity | Finding | Proposed improvement |
|---|---|---|---|
| GF-006 | Medium | Tower targets are about 27–29 CSS pixels wide at 320–360 px viewport widths. | Give each site a 44 px hit area while keeping the marker compact. Check for overlap and accidental taps at the narrowest supported viewport. |
