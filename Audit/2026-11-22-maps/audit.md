# Campaign map and placement audit

Reviewed release ec46433. Evidence comes from source inspection, geometric range checks and deterministic simulations. Player difficulty remains unmeasured.

## GF-44 · One Archer strategy wins every campaign level with full lives

Rank sites by number of road tile centres within 1.8 tiles, open with three Archers and buy another at 30 silver. All 30 levels won at 3× with 3 lives. The fixed three alone won nine. Repeated opportunities to stack damage at high-coverage positions. Compare single-type and mixed strategies on new levels.

## GF-45 · Village Watch can be cleared without reinvestment

SimaYi cleared all five Village Watch levels with two Archers and one Drum and no further input. The original high-coverage cluster handles even the escort. Give a later wave a readable reason to reinvest and test a fixed opening.

## GF-46 · Each chapter repeats the same five wave schedules

The same escort closes level five across the campaign. All battles consume one shared formation schedule. Give chapters distinct threats tied to their geography.

## GF-47 · Route choices repeat across five levels

Four chapters use one lane. Both two-lane chapters merge near the gate. Level progression varies waves while keeping routes fixed. Change one meaningful approach per chapter and preview it before purchases.

## GF-48 · Yiling closures spare attractive positions

The auditor’s fixed opening at sites 19, 36 and 44 avoided every closure and won levels one through three. Withdrawn positions are peripheral to the tested strategy. Warn before closing one attractive bend and test the tradeoff.

## GF-49 · Ferry and supply objectives look like the same gate

Jing departures and Wuzhang deliveries both advance from wave count. The renderer shares a gate and progress lacks a visible world object. Place boats at the waterline and carts on the supply road and show each departure.

## GF-50 · Placement markers present unreachable sites as equivalent

At setup 48 of 80 legal Village Watch sites cannot reach even one point of the road with a base Archer. Legality only tests occupancy of the road and closures, without feedback on useful coverage. Keep free placement and highlight the road segment reached before purchase.
