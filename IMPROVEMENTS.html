Improvement register

Edward and Fengchu use this register to discuss proposed improvements and give KongMing a clear brief once a change is agreed.

How to use it
  1. Proposed. Add one entry per improvement with a stable ID and a short title. Describe what the player should see or be able to do under Expected behavior. Under How to verify, state what to test and the result to look for.
  2. Agreed. After Edward approves the expected behavior, update the entry and mark it Agreed. The queue hands it to KongMing when he is free and its prerequisites are Accepted. Bring material changes back for agreement.
  3. Building. KongMing marks the entry when work starts. Before pausing, add a Next action using a span with class “checkpoint” so the queue can resume from it.
  4. Blocked. Record the specific decision or dependency. Independent agreed work can continue. Return the item to Agreed once resolved and record the next action.
  5. Accepted. Mark the entry Accepted after SimaYi accepts the web change. Record the review ID or accepted commit when available.

For an existing finding, include its GF ID and keep the evidence in the project’s audit ledger.

IMP-01Battle speed toggle

Accepted
Expected behavior
One button beside Pause shows the current speed. Each tap cycles 1× → 2× → 3× → 1× on the battle screen.
How to verify
On a short phone, cycle all speeds, confirm the simulation follows the displayed speed, and check that the board and purchase controls fit without scrolling.
Acceptance
SimaYi approved AUD-026 on ecebf58. Offline touch at 320 × 480 passed all speeds, pause/resume, saved speed and purchase layout checks.

IMP-02Volley Platform

Accepted
Expected behavior
Prototype first on the web in Changban Level 3. A slow-reloading volley hits up to three nearby enemies. Choose Archer + Drum or Archer + Volley in the briefing and keep that choice through Retry and restore. Previously placed Drums remain usable. Reuse existing purchase and save logic.
How to verify
At equal silver, Volley is stronger against clustered targets and Archer is stronger against isolated targets. Check that Drum still helps against fast enemies, that the two-card dock fits without scrolling at 320 × 480, and that the trial works offline.
Acceptance
SimaYi approved AUD-027 on 54a6bb7 for private web playtest. Offline touch, equal-silver scenarios, save, Retry and Level 4 refund checks passed.

IMP-03Heavy Crossbow

Accepted
Expected behavior
Trial after Volley in Jing Level 5. A heavy bolt targets the enemy with the most remaining HP in range. The slow reload rewards placement along a long stretch of road.
How to verify
In a mixed wave, verify the Crossbow prioritizes high-HP enemies, a long stretch allows a second shot, and the Archer remains useful against lighter troops. Reuse the purchase and save systems from the first trial.
Acceptance
SimaYi approved AUD-028 on cd7916b. The Jing Level 5 trial passed targeting, placement, save, Retry and offline touch checks.

IMP-04Watch Beacon

Accepted
Expected behavior
Trial last in Wuzhang. The Beacon speeds the reload of one nearby Archer, creating a choice between supporting a well-placed Archer and using the same site for another tower.
How to verify
Compare a Beacon and Archer pairing with a second tower and the Archer’s Faster fire upgrade. A player can identify the supported Archer and explain when the Beacon is worth the site.
Acceptance
SimaYi approved AUD-029 on 48e9bddccf066ff37b216ca05a57e5de7615bf3f. The Wuzhang Level 3 trial passed placement, reload, save, Retry and live offline touch checks. Uncoached player feedback on the link and placement choice is pending.

Brackeys research proposals

Eight proposals combine the 28 research ideas. The numbers on each card refer to the consolidated ideas in the project’s design notes. Edward approved all eight for planning and build. Start with IMP-05 and take the remaining items in register order.

IMP-05Show the next wave before it starts

Accepted
Expected behavior
During setup and preparation, show attacker counts by type and the first active entrance on maps with several approaches. Derive the preview from the same formation and route data used to spawn the wave. Keep the brief preview in the battle dock and put the full breakdown in Orders.
How to verify
At 320 × 480, compare the preview with actual spawns on one single-route and one split-route mission. Check 1× and 3×, and confirm the dock stays visible without resizing or covering the board.
Acceptance
SimaYi approved AUD-030 on 895f2633e69c9a776e11f0f399a0ad6a8f924fbc. The live preview matched spawns at 1× and 3× with no scrolling or board resizing at 320 × 480.

IMP-06Show what the selected tile allows

Accepted
Expected behavior
Make four tile states clear when they matter: available, useful for the selected defense, occupied, and closing. Show the selected tower’s role and installed upgrades even when it has no upgrades left. Keep the current refund amount and two-card dock.
How to verify
At 320 × 480, select adjacent available and occupied tiles, then try a soon-to-close tile. Verify the player can tell what will happen, selection costs nothing, and taps on the dock or through the preparation ring reach only the intended control or tile.
Acceptance
SimaYi approved AUD-031 on 0ee0b8c716b0c4544c64772516b6a85a44ef289f. Live offline touch checks passed for adjacent and closing tiles, upgrade summaries, preparation-ring targeting, and full refunds.
