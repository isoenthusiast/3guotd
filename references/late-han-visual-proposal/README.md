# Late Han village defense

## What the four references contribute

1. [Fortified riverside map](https://encrypted-tbn0.gstatic.com/images?q=tbn:ANd9GcRxcdtWNMCY5wko0eH0nnOwQEjqeK1MUlqPYLjN0b5g7A&s=10). Walls, water and roofs give the map a specific place. Keep that geographic identity and reserve screen edges for a few combat controls.
2. [Grassland switchbacks](https://gbaike-image.cdn.bcebos.com/2f738bd4b31c87014c9c9b262d7f9e2f0608ffcc/2f738bd4b31c87014c9c9b262d7f9e2f0608ffcc_url?x-bce-process=image/format,f_auto/resize,m_lfit,w_400,limit_1). The pale road is easy to track at a glance. Use a continuous ochre lane, with distinct corner stakes at buildable sites so the road edge and placement cues read separately.
3. [Bright tower combat](https://play-lh.googleusercontent.com/VPi_cfPQq8bRwXTihkkG4eemwocTkIbI6Jh0_DEVDkCLsrIiCBNZ5-QKVNIV0RqfJgXEv_706fVHUp-cDXa1jA=w648-h364-rw). Tower profiles and bright effects make actions legible even at small scale. Give the archer a fine, pale flight trail and let the low, circular drum show a brief ground pulse.
4. [Desert lane defense](https://encrypted-tbn0.gstatic.com/images?q=tbn:ANd9GcQJnQno4xv2NLqsm2Gb1IIovh9FENw0VzWacTu1WW36fSF77Fx9tPIKW-w&s=10). The compact wave and resource cluster lets the map dominate. Keep the status bar small and leave the purchase tray collapsed until a site is selected.

## Village Watch visual direction

Use hand-painted, three-quarter-view art with clay, charcoal roof tiles, reed green and small cinnabar accents. Keep roads and units sharper than vegetation. Reserve the strongest contrast for the target, health bar and shot. Give the gate a heavy timber frame and low earth walls. The archer's raised, tiled watch platform and the drum's squat frame have different silhouettes even in grayscale.

The architecture is a game interpretation of late Eastern Han forms. The [Met's watchtower and front court](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/49520) and the [Smithsonian's two-story watchtower](https://asia-archive.si.edu/object/F1907.68a-d/) anchor the roofs, railings and raised platforms. Infantry carries a round shield, a runner has a narrow silhouette, cavalry reads by its horse, and a commander has a broad armored silhouette and pennant. These are proposed role cues.

The main menu carries mode selection and session controls. The briefing gets the largest illustration and ends with one entry action. The battlefield keeps the complete portrait route and puts the build tray in a shallow contextual strip. During a later layout pass, check the illustration and controls at 320 × 480 using two or more towers and several moving enemies.
