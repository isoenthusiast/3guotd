 is a Beginner Map in Bloons TD 6. This track is featured in the tutorial of the game. It was one of 9 Beginner Maps to be introduced on initial release, and was one of the 21 original maps introduced on initial release.

Ever since the game's release until Version 39.0, Monkey Meadow, Tree Stump, and Town Center were the first three maps to be always shown in the maps menu in chronological order. Since Version 39.0, the order of the pinned maps was changed to the following order: Monkey Meadow, In the Loop, latest Beginner Map, Tree Stump, Town Center, second-latest Beginner Map.

Overview

Town Center takes place in a town, with the stone path appearing as the track for bloons to travel. There are three buildings and a windmill, all of which can disrupt monkey's line of sight. The track is next to the coast, with a shoreline right next to the town.

Bloons enter from the bottom part of the lane on the left, and follow the pathing of the lane. Bloons make a turn around the smaller right-hand building and exit to the right.

Strategy

Summary

Unlike Tree Stump, the obstacles purely serve as an inconvenience not only to just line-of-sight, but blocking placement of towers in what would be rather optimal spots, especially the house at the top right of the map for Tack Shooters. There is water, but it's in an suboptimal spot for towers that have limited range.

Tips

- The water space isn't entirely useless, because there is at least enough distance to place Bloontonium Reactor to decamo the entrance, as well as using the water space for various Merchantmen.
- - You can also place several Monkey Subs with Advanced Intel to counteract the low amount of track space with their base range, as long as other towers cover a significant portion of the track. As this is the better crosspath for Triple guns and above, one could use a Sub strategy here.
- Near the start can do well for a Spike-o-pult for straight-line action.
- Any tower with a footprint radius of 7 and below can fit the space between the two parallel roads that are closest to each other.

Trivia

- A Red Bloon can leak in 30.5 seconds on Hard Difficulty, making Town Center the third shortest Beginner Map next to Monkey Meadow and End of the Road.
- Aquatic buildings could be placed on the crates near the end of the track. This was fixed in update 2.0.0.
- - If there was a saved game with this bug and the player re-enters the save after updating it past 2.0.0, the game will crash.
- Due to the numerous 3D objects and a path shorter than most other beginner tracks, Town Center can be considered the hardest beginner track in the game. The other candidates are Hedge and Lotus Island.
- This is the track featured in the tutorial for BTD6.
- The largest building resembles the Monkey Town Hall from Bloons Monkey City.
