The unnamed tutorial monkey Conjectural name derived from internal data (also called General Monkey) is a non-playable character in Bloons Monkey City and Bloons TD 6. In Bloons Monkey City, they What's Up At Ninja Kiwi? - 11th March, 2022 appear on several menus and, in the Flash version, some loading screens. In Bloons TD 6, they guide the player during the game's tutorial and instruct players in Co-op mode to place towers in their respective sections.

He wears a green beret and a large bushy mustache. He sometimes uses a notepad, sometimes holding a pointer as well. He also gives tips during the Tutorial alongside with Dart Monkeys and other towers beforehand.

Trivia

- Unlike his BMC counterpart, Army Monkey, Tutorial Monkey has a large bushy mustache and his mouth is not visible.
- The monkey who showed up in the BMC Flash track loading screen (along with Army Monkey and Mortar Monkey) and the "Try Again?" dialog box whenever a player loses a MvM defense in BMC Flash could be presumably his first appearances.
- He once again returns in the revamped tutorial in Bloons TD 6. You could replay it again if you forgot the mechanics on placing towers and other strategies.
