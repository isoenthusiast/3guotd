Default

+ Normal Damage occurred by level up: +0.15%

Obtained from: Default Skin (Free)

This battlefield skin buffs the basic attack damage for all your dice on the board.

Final Colosseum

+ Slow Effect occurred by level up: +1.5%

Obtained from: Store (500 Diamonds) / Main Quest -33

The battlefield spawns 1 sand swamp (2 sand swamps in coop) at random location that last(s) for the entire game, slowing the enemies that cross the sand swamps on the track.

Club: Violet

+ Attack Speed Buff occurred by level up: 1.5%

Obtained from: Store (500 Diamonds) / Main Quest -12

2 random spots on the board are marked. Any dice placed on the marked spots will receive an attack speed buff. This buff does not stack with existing attack speed bonus buff from the attack speed buff dice - light and lunar. The final attack speed buff is decided by whichever buff is providing a higher value.

The Pantheon

+ Crit Chance Buff occurred by level up: 1.5%

Obtained from: PvP Reward (9700 Trophies)

2 random spots on the board are marked. Any dice placed on the marked spots will recieve a critical chance buff. This buff does not stack with existing critical chance bonus buff from the critical chance buff dice - critical and lunar. The final critical chance buff is decided by whichever buff is providing a higher value.
