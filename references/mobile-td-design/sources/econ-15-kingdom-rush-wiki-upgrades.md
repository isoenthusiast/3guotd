thumb|168pxUpgrades in the Kingdom Rush series are used to increase the power and effectiveness of the player's arsenal.

## Differences between games

In Kingdom Rush, Kingdom Rush: Frontiers and Kingdom Rush: Origins, there are six upgrade trees, one for each tower type and one for each spell, and each tree has a total of five linear upgrades. Upgrades are bought with stars.

In Kingdom Rush: Vengeance and Kingdom Rush 5: Alliance, there are four upgrade trees. In both games, three trees improve towers, heroes and Call Reinforcements; the fourth tree improves Soul Impact in Vengeance or each faction (Linirean and Dark Army)'s assets in Alliance. The Reinforcements tree branches into two mutually-exclusive branches: the first improves their stats in melee combat, the second gives them ranged weapons to attack enemies, including flying ones. Upgrades are bought with upgrade points.

In Kingdom Rush 6: Genesis, there are four upgrade trees that improve towers (one per tower type) and are bought with upgrade points. Every hero and spell has its own upgrade tree that are bought with skill points. As heroes and spells are used, they earn experience over time and gain skill points with each level-up. The number of upgrades in each tree is 7 (for towers), 11 (for heroes) and 8 (for spells). Each hero's tree has one pair of mutually-exclusive upgrades; each tower type and spell's has two mutually-exclusive branches of two upgrades each.

## Stars and Upgrade Points

## Stars

In the first three games, where upgrades are bought with stars, they can be obtained in several ways:
- Completing a Campaign level gives stars based on the number of lives left:
- 18-20 lives: 3 stars
- 6-17 lives: 2 stars
- 1-5 lives: 1 star
- Completing a Heroic Challenge or Iron Challenge gives 1 star.
- Exclusive to the Flash version:
- Buying the Premium Content gives 15 stars.
- Clicking on the Twitter and Facebook buttons gives 1 star each.
Because of this, a player needs to complete Heroic, Iron Challenges and/or elite stages in order to fully upgrade their arsenal.

## Upgrade Points

Upgrade points are exclusively earned from Campaign mode; completing other modes (Heroic Challenge, Iron Challenge, Blitz Challenge, Spell Rush, Hero Party and Classic Rush) give no upgrade points. The number of upgrade points earned is independent of stars; no matter how many lives the player finishes the stage with, the number of upgrade points is always the same. Most stages grant three upgrade points upon completion, with the exception of the following.

In Kingdom Rush: Vengeance:
- Kazan Mines, Jokull's Nest: 4 upgrade points
- Otil Farmlands: 6 upgrade points
- Denas's Castle: 10 upgrade points
In Kingdom Rush 5: Alliance:
- Sea of Trees: no upgrade points
- The Wildbeast Den: 6 upgrade points
- The Eyesore Tower: 5 upgrade points
- Canyon Plateau, Hunger's Peak: 8 upgrade points
All trees can be fully upgraded once the entire main campaign is completed.
