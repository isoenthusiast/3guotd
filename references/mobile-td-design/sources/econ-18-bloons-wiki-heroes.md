Heroes (known as Characters in Bloons Adventure Time TD) are a special type of tower that were introduced with Bloons TD 6. Heroes are unique characters that have their own personality, voice lines, abilities and attacks. The major difference from regular towers is that Heroes and Characters have a unique leveling system, and only a limited amount of them can be equipped and used per game.

In both Bloons TD 6 and Bloons TD Battles 2, only one Hero may be equipped and placed per player per game. In addition, Heroes do not have the conventional upgrade system. Rather, they have a single upgrade path, with individual tiers being split into smaller "levels" that level based on Hero XP (distinct from Tower and Rank XP), which is accumulated automatically as rounds progress. In co-op each player may have one Hero, so XP is split evenly among players.

In Bloons Adventure Time TD, each Character has their own upgrade system, leveling currency, weapon type, eligible equippable trinkets and allies, and attacking styles.

In Bloons Pop!, the Heroes are adaptations of their BTD6 counterparts and are automatically unlocked and first in queue upon reaching a certain amount of points.

In Bloons Card Storm, the Hero is the primary character for each player, with a unique set of abilities that consume Bloontonium to use.

## Trivia
- Gwendolin, Quincy (in his Cyber Quincy skin), Ezili, Pat Fusty, Etienne, and Corvus are the only heroes that call out a B.A.D. by its name.
- Among all the heroes that call out a M.O.A.B. by its name, Striker Jones, Etienne and Lifeguard Brickell say 'M-O-A-B', while all other heroes pronounce MOAB as 'Mow-AHB'.
- Heroes say different voice lines when wearing an alternate skin.
- Pat Fusty has the shortest range of all of the heroes.
- Adora has the longest displayed range of any hero when her Long Arm of Light ability is active.
- Only in BTD6 can heroes be tapped to annoy them.
- Pat Fusty is the first in Bloons TD 6 to introduce amphibious game mechanics. Soon, more towers gain this, including Mermonkey and Ice Monkey.
- All Heroes first released in 2024 are female: Rosalia, Bonnie, and Amelia.

- Adora and Quincy hate spicy food the most.
- According to Ninja Kiwi's [https://ninjakiwi.com/blog/news/173445-what-s-up-at-ninja-kiwi-3rd-september-2021?mobile=0 3rd September 2021 blog], Captain Churchill is the best hero at chess.
- According to Ninja Kiwi's [https://ninjakiwi.com/blog/news/173449-what-s-up-at-ninja-kiwi-1st-october-2021 1st October 2021 blog], Dr. Monkey messing with the multiverse is the reason why having multiple heroes per game is possible. On the same blog, they also said that turning off hero audio basically makes heroes simulate a playing game of "who can be the most quiet?", which is the canonical lore to this in-game feature.
- According to Ninja Kiwi's [https://ninjakiwi.com/blog/news/173450-what-s-up-at-ninja-kiwi-8th-october-2021?mobile=0 8th October 2021 blog], they work with a voice talent agency for most of their voice acting work. They are found [https://www.bigmouthvoices.com/ here].
- According to Ninja Kiwi's [https://ninjakiwi.com/blog/news/173455-what-s-up-at-ninja-kiwi-12th-november-2021?mobile=0 November 12th 2021 blog], each of the heroes have a preferred method of transportation. The favored transportation methods for Churchill and Brickell are obvious by judging their vehicles, but Striker likes parachuting and Quincy likes walking.
- The same blog also states that Ninja Kiwi is hoping to release some Hero plushies.
- According to Ninja Kiwi's [https://ninjakiwi.com/blog/news/173458-what-s-up-at-ninja-kiwi-3rd-december-2021?mobile=0 December 3rd 2021 blog], there is a possibility for non-monkey heroes to come to Bloons TD 6, but they had also stated that Heroes must "be packed to overflowing with character."
- According to Ninja Kiwi's [https://ninjakiwi.com/blog/news/173478-what-s-up-at-ninja-kiwi-3rd-june-2022 3rd June 2022 blog], all Heroes canonically have trained during the events of Bloons TD 5.
