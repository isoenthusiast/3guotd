## Description

```
When designing Duskers I focused on creating as much variety as I could within a run, and while that made runs different, it didn’t make them feel distinct. For Below the Crown, I focused on making runs feel distinct from each other, but there are some tradeoffs when you focus on Inter-run variety vs Intra-run variety. This talk discusses those tradeoffs and will help designers make a conscious choice for their game based on how they want it to feel.
```
